WELCOME TO BULIIGO PRIMARY

Buliigo Boarding and Day Primary School is a premier institution in Iganga, Uganda, focused on academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth. Our nurturing environment with dedicated teachers ensures each child's success.

OUR MISSION

A Firm Steady Education Foundation through: promoting a reading and writing culture by age 5, promoting social-cultural activities, promoting leadership roles and, above all, maintaining academic excellence.

OUR MOTTO

"Appreciation Builds Interest" — building character and competence in every learner.

OUR VALUES

Appreciation, Interest, Confidence, Performance and Acceptance. Every child is unique in their own way, which is why we promote individual learning in small clusters of children.

Message from the Head Teacher

Dear Parents and Guardians,

On behalf of the staff at Buliigo Boarding and Day Primary School, I warmly welcome you to our school community. Since our establishment in 2008, we have remained committed to providing quality education that shapes the whole child — academically, spiritually, and socially.

Our dedicated team of teachers works tirelessly to ensure every child reaches their full potential. We believe in a balanced education that combines academic rigor with character development, sports, and creative arts. Our boarding facilities provide a safe, caring, and home-like environment for our learners.

We invite you to partner with us in this noble task of raising responsible and successful citizens. Together, we can build a brighter future for our children.

With pride and commitment,


Head Teacher - Buliigo Primary School
