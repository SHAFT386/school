About Buliigo Primary School

Excellence with Integrity — shaping future leaders since 2008.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that nurtures the intellectual, social, and spiritual growth of every child, preparing them for a successful and responsible life.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of educational excellence in Iganga and Uganda, shaping future leaders who transform communities with integrity and compassion.

Why Buliigo Primary?

  • Quality Education: Blending national curriculum with modern teaching methods
  • Dedicated Teachers: Highly trained, passionate educators
  • Character Development: Values, discipline, and leadership training
  • Modern Facilities: Spacious classrooms, library, playgrounds, and boarding facilities
  • Holistic Growth: Sports, arts, and extracurricular activities

Our Signature Programs

Inspiring young minds through academics, arts, and athletics

Academics

STEM, languages, and humanities with modern teaching methods, small class sizes, and personalized tutoring for every child.

Creative Arts

Music, drama, visual arts, and cultural festivals. Students showcase talent at school events and community gatherings.

Sports & Leadership

Football, netball, athletics, and leadership camps. Building teamwork, resilience, and healthy lifestyles.

Our Heritage & Growth

Buliigo Boarding and Day Primary School was founded in 2008 with a dream to bring quality education to Buliigo Village, Iganga. Starting with a small group of dedicated teachers and students, we have grown into a thriving school community of over 500 learners.

We combine local values with global perspectives, ensuring every child receives a well-rounded education that prepares them for success in life.
